Help shape HAP-1 in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Healthcare-Agent-Protocol/healthcare-agent-protocol/discussions).
Bring a question, describe an RCM workflow, or suggest a change to the draft.

## Join the HAP-1 organization

We'd like contributors to join the [HAP-1 GitHub organization](https://github.com/Healthcare-Agent-Protocol).
[Start a discussion](https://github.com/Healthcare-Agent-Protocol/healthcare-agent-protocol/discussions/new) to introduce yourself and request an invitation.
Tell us which RCM workflows you work on and how you'd like to help.
An organization owner can then invite your GitHub account.

You can take part in public discussions before joining the organization.

## Discuss an idea

Use Discussions for questions, workflow examples, design proposals, and feedback on the draft.

Use invented examples only. Never submit patient data, production records, customer scripts, credentials, or internal exports.

For a proposed protocol change, describe:

- The business problem and affected participants.
- The proposed behavior and authority boundaries.
- Changes to messages, schemas, versions, or bindings.
- Failure cases, retry behavior, and conformance checks.
- Compatibility with existing drafts.

When you're ready to change the documentation or code, open a pull request and link the relevant discussion.

## Review the contract

Separate design proposals from normative requirements.
Use MUST, SHOULD, and MAY only when defining a reviewed contract.
Do not claim vendor support or deployment without evidence and permission.

HAP-1 profiles, business contracts, schemas, and examples are pinned to `1.0-draft.1`.
Keep this version during the current draft review.
Change the draft number only when maintainers explicitly decide to publish a new draft.
