# Contributing

Propose focused changes through issues and pull requests.

Use invented examples only. Never submit patient data, production records, customer scripts, credentials, or internal exports.

For a protocol change, describe:

- The business problem and affected participants.
- The proposed behavior and authority boundaries.
- Changes to messages, schemas, versions, or bindings.
- Failure cases, retry behavior, and conformance checks.
- Compatibility with existing drafts.

Separate design proposals from normative requirements.
Use MUST, SHOULD, and MAY only when defining a reviewed contract.
Do not claim vendor support or deployment without evidence and permission.

The supplied HAP-1 document retains its own draft version.
Changes to its wire contract need an explicit version decision.

Contributions use the repository's [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
